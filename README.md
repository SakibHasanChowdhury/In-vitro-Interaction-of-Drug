# In-vitro-Interaction-of-Drug
In Vitro interaction of Amoxicillin with Vildaglaptin and Investigation of Antimicrobial Activity

Preparations
 
● Preparation of Buffer Solution
●Preparation of Stock Solution(Amoxicillin)
●Preparation of Stock Solution(Vildagliptin)

Method

●Preparation of Standard Curve of Amoxicillin
➢Amoxicillin stock solution at pH 1.75 and concentration of 1×10 ̄5 M was added in different concentrations to ten test tubes, to have the following concentrations : 9×10 ̄5M , 8× 10 ̄5M ,7×10 ̄5M ,6×10 ̄5M ,5×10 ̄5M ,4×10 ̄5 M ,3×10 ̄5M ,2×10 ̄5M ,1×10 ̄5M
➢The solutions were then properly mixed . The absorbance values of the solutions were determined at 228.71 nm by UV spectrophotometer. As a control of reference sample, buffer solution of pH 1.75 was used. The standard curve was obtained by plotting the absorbance values against the corresponding concentrations
